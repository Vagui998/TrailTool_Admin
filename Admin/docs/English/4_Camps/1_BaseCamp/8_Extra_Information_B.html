In a transdisciplinary collaboration, there are many differences between team members. These differences are often perceived as challenging — but they can also be leveraged. By recognizing, valuing, and actively using diversity, you can improve both collaboration and outcomes. 

According to Smeenk (2023), three key dimensions in which team members differ in transdisciplinary collaboration are: 

  1. Interest 

  1. Power 

  1. Knowledge 

On the practice page, you’ll already find some real-world experiences and tools that can help.
