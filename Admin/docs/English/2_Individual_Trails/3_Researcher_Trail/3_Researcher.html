Reconsidering Academia: Navigating the Future through Transdisciplinary Work 

Our society and planet face significant challenges that require new ways of thinking. These 'wicked problems'—complex issues that cannot be solved easily - demand collaboration across disciplines and institutions. 

Researchers play a crucial role in this process. They can set goals, choose methods, and evaluate results to understand and improve the world. By including educational and societal partners in research teams, students and partners also develop a research mindset for tackling complexity. 

New Skills and Competencies 

Transdisciplinary education focuses on addressing complex problems and creating impact. Through perspective shifts, you develop skills such as empathy, collaboration, and open-mindedness. The collaborative approach ensures that all participants benefit and learn, in order to fundamentally transform the world.
