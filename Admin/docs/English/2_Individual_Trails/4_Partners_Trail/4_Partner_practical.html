Collaboration depends on the network of the initiator. Research shows that external partners are often recruited from the knowledge circle of teachers, researchers, or students (Vinckx, van Loon & Vleuten, 2022). Sometimes, collaborations are geographically connected, as with the Urban Living Lab Breda in the south of the Netherlands. 

Another method is an open call, such as the Academic Consultancy Training (ACT) - WUR of Wageningen University (NL). Companies and societal organizations can also establish sustainable collaborations with existing transdisciplinary educational settings. 

Reference 

Vinckx K., van Loon S., van der Vleuten E. Succesfactoren voor co-creatie, onderzoeksrapport september 2022
