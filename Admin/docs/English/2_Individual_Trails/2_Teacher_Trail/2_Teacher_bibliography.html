Currently, several examples can already be found in the Netherlands. Both universities of applied sciences and research universities offer transdisciplinary courses. A few examples of transdisciplinary education:

  • In the minor Act for Change Together at Avans University of Applied Sciences, students from various disciplines collaborate with stakeholders on societal issues related to the new economy.
  • At NHL Stenden University of Applied Sciences, students can participate in the Social Innovation Lab project, located in the heart of a neighborhood, where they work directly with residents to apply their practice collaboratively.
  • In the master's insert Shaping Responsible Futures at the University of Twente, students work together with municipalities, NGOs, or companies on issues concerning responsible futures and systemic change.
  • In the elective course Placemaking at the University of Amsterdam, students from different backgrounds engage with local residents and stakeholders to tackle urban challenges and co-create solutions.
