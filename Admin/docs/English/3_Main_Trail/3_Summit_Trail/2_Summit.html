Although there is no guarantee of a predefined result, transdisciplinary collaboration can lead to a wide range of project outcomes because it is driven by learning. It may result in innovative solutions to local challenges such as water usage, a pitch or proposal to local officials, a conference for local stakeholders, a game, a film, a comic, a podcast, a website, etc. These are all tangible outcomes of the project that can be assessed based on their direct impact. 

The theory page discusses various types of tangible outcomes, and the practice page presents some examples.
