The direction in which the team explores solutions is not predetermined. The goal of transdisciplinary learning is to be innovative, and this carries the risk that the team may not make much progress toward a solution. If they choose to stay with the problem longer, this can also be highly valuable, as it allows for a deeper understanding of the issue from multiple perspectives. Often, solutions to certain problems are ineffective because the problem itself is misunderstood. Therefore, understanding the problem—or recognizing its complexity—can be a crucial outcome.
