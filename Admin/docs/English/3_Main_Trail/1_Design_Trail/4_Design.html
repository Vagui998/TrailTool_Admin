The role of the teacher in transdisciplinary education fundamentally differs from the traditional teaching role. The teacher is no longer just a transmitter of knowledge, but a facilitator of learning processes that take place at the intersection of disciplines and society. Transdisciplinary education revolves around jointly exploring and addressing complex societal challenges, and that places different demands on a teacher. 

 

Core Roles of the Teacher 

In this type of education, the teacher takes on multiple — often simultaneous — roles: 

  • Facilitator of learning – guiding students in their learning process and encourage self-regulation 

  • Mediator between disciplines and society – connecting academic knowledge with practical knowledge from external partners 

  • Coach for personal and professional development – supporting students in developing skills for collaboration, reflection, and dealing with complexity
