Transdisciplinary education involves two types of environments: 

  • The educational and research context (the university or university of applied sciences) 

  • And the external context, involving partners such as the professional field and broader society. 

This includes both the soft side of collaboration and the physical locations where the work takes place. It’s important to determine how these contexts are connected within the transdisciplinary module. This influences what and how people learn and work in transdisciplinary education. 

On the Theory page, you’ll find explanations of how to understand these environments and the forms they can take. 

Under the Practice tab, you’ll find examples of existing projects and how they’ve designed their learning environments.
