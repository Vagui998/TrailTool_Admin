Exercise: From Societal Issue to Learnable Challenge 

Based on the societal issue “Diversity in Social Housing” from the Stadsacademie Gent Block, T., Prové, C., Dehaene, M. et al. Understanding urban sustainability from Mode 2 Science and transdisciplinary education: how Master Thesis Ateliers of the Ghent Stadsacademie tackle wicked issues. Environment, Development and Sustainability (2022). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10668-022-02657-0 

 

Step 1 – Choose a Concrete Societal Issue 

Select a current issue from a region, neighborhood, or organization. Choose something that is complex, has multiple perspectives, and is socially relevant. 

Tip: Think of themes that connect to your educational practice or to partners in the field. 

Example: 

Access to social housing for people with a migration background in Ghent is difficult, despite formal equality in the regulations. 


Step 2 – Analyze the Issue Using Four Learnability Aspects 

Answer the questions below to deepen your understanding of the issue. Write your answers briefly and concretely. 

 

Step 3 – Formulate a Learnable Challenge 

Use an open-ended formulation that invites research or design. For example: 

  • How can… 

  • What is needed to… 

  • In what way can… 

Example: 

How can the allocation system for social housing in Ghent better reflect the diversity of prospective tenants, especially people with a migration background, given the tensions between formal equality, selection criteria, and perceived inequality?
